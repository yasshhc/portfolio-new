Featured case study

CSM Assistant: from copy-pasting transcripts to a churn radar

Problem

After every client call I pasted the transcript into ChatGPT. No memory per client, and no way to see patterns across accounts.

Insight

The valuable output wasn't the email. It was pain points and churn signals, compared across every client.

What I built

Fathom sync, two AI passes per meeting, a task list per client, and a cross-client dashboard of pain points and churn signals.

Outcome
